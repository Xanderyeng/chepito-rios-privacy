Privacy Policy

Chepito Rios Online Radio · Effective 22 October 2026

The app does not require an account, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share your data with advertisers. It streams audio from our radio station and lets you optionally send us feedback by email.

Chepito Rios Online Radio (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an internet radio streaming app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices. It is short, because the App does very little with your data.

Information We Collect

Information you choose to give us. If you use the Feedback feature, you can enter your name, email address, subject, and message. Submitting the form opens your device’s own email app with that information pre-filled. The App does not collect, store, or transmit this information. It is sent only if and when you choose to send the resulting email yourself, from your own email account to ours.

Information collected automatically. None. The App does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software development kits (SDKs). It does not collect device identifiers, location data, browsing history, or usage analytics, and it does not use App Tracking Transparency or any cross-app tracking.

To play the radio stream and show what is currently playing, the App makes network requests to our streaming provider and to our own backend service, which returns the current track’s title, artist, and cover art. These requests carry no account, identifier, or personal information about you. As with any internet request, the receiving servers can see the IP address the request came from as part of ordinary network operation. We do not use this to identify you, do not build user profiles, and do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Permissions

Notifications (Android). Used only to show the playback notification and lock-screen controls while audio is playing.

Microphone. The App declares a microphone usage description because the underlying audio playback system can request it. The App never records audio and never accesses your microphone. You will not be asked for microphone permission in normal use.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on a third-party streaming service to deliver the live audio, and on our own backend to relay publicly available track metadata (title, artist, cover art). No personal data passes through that backend. We do not use third-party advertising networks or analytics services.

Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, for example through the Feedback form, please contact us below so we can address it.

Data Retention

The App does not itself collect or store personal information, so there is no App-side retention period. Any email you choose to send us is retained according to our normal business email practices.

Your Choices

You can use the App without submitting any personal information; the Feedback form is entirely optional. Uninstalling the App removes locally cached data, such as your volume setting, from your device. You can change notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date above when we do. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
